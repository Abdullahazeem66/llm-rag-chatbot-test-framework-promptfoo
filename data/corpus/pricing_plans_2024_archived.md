# Pricing and Plans (2024) - Archived

This page describes pricing that was in effect from March 1, 2024 until January 14, 2026. It has been replaced by the 2026 pricing. It is kept for reference for customers on legacy contracts.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly price (per user) | Annual price (per user, per month) | Max members | Storage (per workspace) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 3 | 1 GB |
| Team | $6 | $5.10 | 20 | 50 GB |
| Business | $12 | $10.20 | Unlimited | 500 GB |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Annual billing gave a 15% discount compared to monthly billing.

## Free trial

New workspaces could start a 30-day free trial of the Business plan. A credit card was required to start the trial.

## Legacy customers

Customers who were on the Team or Business plan before January 15, 2026 keep their legacy price until their next renewal after January 15, 2027. The Team plan was renamed Starter and the Business plan was renamed Pro in release 5.0.
