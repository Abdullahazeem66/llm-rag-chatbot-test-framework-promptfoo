# Brindlework Product Overview

Brindlework is a project management and team collaboration platform for teams of every size. It was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Lisbon, Portugal. Brindlework is available on the web, as desktop apps for Windows and macOS, and as mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Core concepts

A **workspace** is the top-level container for an organization. Each workspace has its own members, billing, and settings. A workspace contains **projects**, and each project contains **tasks**. Tasks can have assignees, due dates, priorities (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), subtasks, comments, and file attachments.

Projects can be viewed in several ways:

- **List view** shows tasks as a sortable, filterable list. Available on all plans.
- **Board view** shows tasks as cards in Kanban columns. Available on all plans.
- **Calendar view** shows tasks by due date. Available on all plans.
- **Timeline view** (Gantt chart) shows task durations and dependencies. Available on Pro and Enterprise plans only.

## Recurring tasks

Tasks can repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or on a custom schedule. When a recurring task is completed, the next occurrence is created automatically. Recurring tasks were introduced in version 4.8.

## Automations

Automations run actions when a trigger fires, for example "when a task moves to Done, notify the project owner". Automation runs are limited per workspace per month:

- Free: automations are not available
- Starter: 250 runs per month
- Pro: 5,000 runs per month
- Enterprise: unlimited runs

Unused runs do not carry over to the next month. When the limit is reached, automations pause until the next billing month begins.

## Brindlework AI

Brindlework AI can summarize long task comment threads and generate weekly project status reports. Brindlework AI is included in Pro and Enterprise plans and was released in version 5.2. Workspace admins can turn Brindlework AI off in Settings > Workspace > AI features. Customer content processed by Brindlework AI is not used to train AI models.

## Guests

Guests are external collaborators, such as clients or contractors, who can access only the projects they are invited to. Guests cannot see the member directory or create new projects. The number of guests allowed depends on the plan (see Pricing and Plans).
