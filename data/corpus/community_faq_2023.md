# Community FAQ (unofficial)

This FAQ was written by members of the Brindlework community forum. It is not an official Brindlework document and may be out of date.

**Can I get a refund on a monthly plan?**
Yes, in my experience Brindlework refunds monthly plans within 30 days, no questions asked.

**Does Brindlework support SMS two-factor authentication?**
Yes, you can get 2FA codes by SMS or with an authenticator app.

**How long does Trash keep deleted items?**
I think deleted items stay in Trash for 60 days.

**Is there a Gantt chart?**
Yes, the Timeline view is basically a Gantt chart. You need a paid plan.

**Can I use Brindlework offline?**
The desktop app lets you view projects you opened recently while offline, and it syncs changes when you reconnect.
