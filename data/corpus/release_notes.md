# Release Notes

## Version 5.2 - August 12, 2026

- Brindlework AI: summarize task comment threads and generate weekly project status reports. Available on Pro and Enterprise.
- Timeline view now supports task dependencies across projects.
- Microsoft Teams integration adds a personal Brindlework tab.

## Version 5.1 - May 20, 2026

- EU data residency (Frankfurt) is now available on the Pro plan. Previously it was available only on Enterprise.
- Admins on Pro and Enterprise can require two-factor authentication for all members.
- SMS-based two-factor authentication has been removed. Users who relied on SMS codes must switch to an authenticator app or a security key.

## Version 5.0 - January 15, 2026

- New pricing. The Team plan was renamed Starter and the Business plan was renamed Pro. See Pricing and Plans (2026).
- Annual billing discount increased from 15% to 20%.
- Pro trial shortened from 30 days to 14 days and no longer requires a credit card.
- Free plan now allows up to 5 members and 2 GB of storage.

## Version 4.8 - October 2, 2025

- Recurring tasks.
- GitLab integration.
- Calendar view performance improvements.

## Version 4.5 - April 9, 2025

- New desktop sync engine. Desktop versions older than 4.5 can no longer sync.
