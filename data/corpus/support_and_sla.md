# Support Channels and Response Times

Business hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC, excluding Portuguese public holidays.

| Plan | Support channels | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Help center and community forum | No guaranteed response |
| Starter | Email | Within 48 business hours |
| Pro | Email and live chat | Within 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | Email, live chat, and 24/7 phone | 1 hour for P1 (critical) issues, 4 hours for P2 |

## Priority levels (Enterprise)

- **P1 (critical):** Brindlework is unavailable or data loss is occurring for many users.
- **P2 (high):** A major feature is not working and there is no workaround.
- **P3 (normal):** A feature is partly impaired or there is a question about usage. Target response is 1 business day.

Enterprise customers also get a dedicated customer success manager and quarterly business reviews.

## Uptime commitment

Brindlework targets 99.9% monthly uptime for all paid plans. Enterprise contracts include a 99.95% uptime SLA with service credits: 10% of the monthly fee if uptime falls below 99.95%, and 25% if it falls below 99.0%. Service status is published at status.brindlework.example.

## Contacting support

Email support@brindlework.example or use the chat button in the app (Pro and Enterprise). Enterprise customers can find the 24/7 phone number in Settings > Support.
