# Data Retention and Privacy

## Trash

Deleted tasks, projects, and files are moved to the Trash. Items in the Trash are kept for 30 days and can be restored by the person who deleted them or by a workspace Admin. After 30 days they are permanently deleted. Enterprise workspaces can configure Trash retention of up to 180 days.

## Exporting data

Workspace Owners and Admins can export all workspace data at any time in Settings > Workspace > Export. Exports are available in CSV and JSON formats and include tasks, comments, and project structure. File attachments are exported as a separate ZIP archive. Export links expire after 7 days.

## Deleting a workspace or account

Only a workspace Owner can delete a workspace. After deletion, workspace data is permanently removed from production systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Deletion cannot be undone, so export your data first.

Individual users can delete their personal account in Profile > Account. Tasks and comments they created stay in the workspace but are shown as created by "Deleted user".

## Data residency

Workspaces are hosted in the United States by default. An EU region (Frankfurt, Germany) is available for Pro and Enterprise workspaces. The region is selected when the workspace is created. Moving an existing workspace to a different region is only available to Enterprise customers and is done by the support team.

## Compliance

Brindlework complies with GDPR and offers a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which can be signed online in Settings > Workspace > Legal. Brindlework is SOC 2 Type II certified. Brindlework does not sell customer data, and customer content is not used to train AI models.
