# Billing and Refunds

## How billing works

Brindlework bills per member seat. Guests do not count as paid seats. Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month as the original purchase. Annual plans are charged once per year, upfront.

Invoices are available in Settings > Billing > Invoices and are also emailed to the billing contact. You can add a VAT or tax ID and a company address to invoices from the same page. Changes to invoice details apply only to future invoices.

## Payment methods

Brindlework accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Payment by bank transfer against an invoice is available for Enterprise customers and for annual Pro subscriptions with 20 or more seats.

## Adding and removing seats

When you add members in the middle of a billing period, you are charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in that period. When you remove members, the unused amount is added as account credit and applied to your next invoice. Account credit is not refunded as cash.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Brindlework retries the charge 3 times over 7 days and emails the billing contact after each failed attempt. If the payment still has not succeeded 14 days after the first failure, the workspace becomes read-only. Access is restored immediately after the outstanding balance is paid.

## Refund policy

- **Monthly subscriptions** can be refunded in full within 14 days of the most recent charge.
- **Annual subscriptions** can be refunded on a prorated basis within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable, but you can turn off auto-renewal at any time.
- Charges for extra storage add-ons are not refundable.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

To request a refund, contact billing@brindlework.example from the email address of a workspace Owner or billing admin, and include your workspace URL.

## Cancelling

You can cancel a paid plan in Settings > Billing > Plan. After cancelling, the workspace stays on the paid plan until the end of the current billing period and then moves to the Free plan.
