# Pricing and Plans

These prices are effective from January 15, 2026 (release 5.0). All prices are in US dollars, per user, and exclude applicable taxes such as VAT.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly price (per user) | Annual price (per user, per month) | Max members | Storage (per workspace) | Guests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 5 | 2 GB | 0 |
| Starter | $8 | $6.40 | 25 | 100 GB | 5 |
| Pro | $15 | $12 | Unlimited | 1 TB | 50 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared to monthly billing. Annual plans are billed upfront for the full year.

## Free trial

New workspaces can start a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is required to start the trial. At the end of the trial, the workspace automatically moves to the Free plan unless a paid plan is selected. If the workspace has more than 5 members when it moves to the Free plan, it becomes read-only until members are removed or a paid plan is chosen.

## Nonprofit and education discounts

Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive a 50% discount on Starter and Pro plans. Apply through the form in Settings > Billing > Discounts. Verification usually takes up to 5 business days.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing is based on the number of users and required features. Enterprise includes SSO, SCIM provisioning, custom data retention, and a dedicated customer success manager. Contact the sales team at sales@brindlework.example to get a quote. The minimum Enterprise contract is 50 users billed annually.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. See Billing and Refunds for how proration works.
