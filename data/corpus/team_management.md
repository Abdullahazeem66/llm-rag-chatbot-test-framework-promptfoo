# Team and Workspace Management

## Roles

Each workspace member has one role:

- **Owner:** full control, including billing, deleting the workspace, and transferring ownership. A workspace can have up to 3 Owners.
- **Admin:** manages members, integrations, and workspace settings, but cannot delete the workspace or change the plan.
- **Member:** creates and works on projects and tasks they have access to.
- **Guest:** external collaborator with access only to projects they are invited to.

Billing admins are Admins who have also been given billing permission; they can view invoices and update payment methods.

## Inviting people

Owners and Admins can invite people by email in Settings > Members > Invite. Invitations expire after 7 days. On the Starter plan and above, admins can also allow anyone with an email address from an approved domain to join the workspace automatically.

## Removing members

When a member is removed, their tasks remain in the workspace and stay assigned to them until reassigned. Removed members lose access immediately. On paid plans, removing a member frees their seat and creates account credit for the unused period.

## Transferring ownership

An Owner can make another Admin an Owner in Settings > Members. If the only Owner leaves the company without transferring ownership, an Admin can request an ownership transfer by contacting support. Support verifies the request with the billing contact before transferring ownership, which usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Multiple workspaces

One Brindlework account can belong to several workspaces. Each workspace is billed separately. You can switch between workspaces from the workspace menu in the top-left corner.
